# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We keep this project a practical, respectful place for engineers and content teams.

**Expected**

- Discuss the work, not the person. Numbers and reproducible evidence beat opinions — that is
  also how our QA gate works.
- State what was actually tested. If a claim is an observation rather than a measured result,
  say so (we do this throughout `docs/`).
- Keep diffs small and explain what changed; report failures instead of hiding them.
- Respect the local-only / no-credentials rule in `SECURITY.md`.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing anyone's private material, credentials, or client assets.
- Fabricated benchmarks, fake QA scores, or "looks fine" approvals without evidence.
- Sustained off-topic promotion or spam.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any project-related public space.

## Reporting

Open a private report through GitHub Security Advisories for sensitive matters, or contact the
maintainers via an issue without details. Reports are handled discreetly; we do not publish
reporter identities.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this standard, and will state
the reason in the thread. Serious or repeated violations can lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
